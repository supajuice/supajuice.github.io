How it started

  1. 01

    A recipe from a friend

    I was away and wanted a simple way to make my own fuel for long runs. A friend sent me an easy recipe, so I gave it a go.

  2. 02

    Tested on the long ones

    I took them on my long runs and endurance runs. No stomach issues, and my energy stayed good the whole way.

  3. 03

    Back home in Cape Town

    I tried the gels in the shops and everything tasted so artificial. It all felt like race day, like running had to be serious. So I brought the recipe home.

Running doesn’t have to be so serious. It should be fun.

Supa Humans

Every gel gives back

Sport is a big passion of mine: running, lifting, all of it. I want to help people reach their goals.

The biggest passion, though, is helping people who need real impact in their lives. So I started Supa Humans, a non-profit company. R1 from every gel sold goes to it, to fund social impact projects.
