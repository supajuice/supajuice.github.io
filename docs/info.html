Delivery

Gels are made fresh every week, so we make your batch, then get it to you one of three ways.

  • Free collection

    Free

    Collect from us in Sea Point, Cape Town, at a time you pick when you order.

  • To your door

    From R99

    Door-to-door with The Courier Guy. The price depends on where you are, so we send you the final fee after you order.

  • To a Pudo locker

    From R90

    R90 for up to 15 gels, R120 for up to 40. We send it to the Pudo locker nearest your address, and you get an SMS with a code to open it.

Where we deliver
Anywhere in South Africa.
Delivery fee
Pudo locker fees are added to your invoice. Door delivery depends on where you are: we send you the final fee after you order, before you pay it. For very big orders we quote you a price. Some discount codes give free delivery.
When it arrives
We send your order once it’s paid and your batch is made. Most deliveries arrive 2–4 working days after we send them. We let you know when it’s on its way.
Minimum order
5 gels.
When it lands
Store your gels in the freezer.
02

Refunds & cancellations

Happy, or your money back. Simple as that.

  1. Changed your mind?

    Cancel any time before your batch is made and we refund you in full. Once it’s made, it can’t be cancelled, because it’s fresh food made just for you.

  2. Not happy with your gels?

    Tell us within 7 days of getting them and we refund what you paid for the gels. No need to send them back.

  3. Damaged, wrong or missing?

    Tell us within 48 hours, with a photo if you can. We replace your order or refund you in full, delivery included.

  4. How refunds are paid

    Back the way you paid (bank transfer or card), within 7 working days of us agreeing the refund.

To cancel or ask for a refund, email or phone us (below) with your invoice number.
